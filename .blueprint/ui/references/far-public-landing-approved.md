## Purpose

This reference governs the **public/customer-facing visual direction** for the Insurance Claims Legacy Modernization MVP. It is a visual guide, not a functional requirements source.

The approved direction is:

- preserve the FAR Seguros logo and core brand identity;
- use FAR cyan/turquoise and yellow accent colors with accessible supporting shades;
- modern, clean, attractive public experience with strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, rounded cards and restrained shadows;
- customer-facing hero/marketing treatment is allowed on WEB-001;
- the public experience and administrative/backoffice experience must remain visually related but structurally distinct;
- the backoffice must prioritize operational density, tables/lists, claim state, evidence and workflow clarity rather than marketing hero imagery;
- do **not** copy the current `farseguros.com.uy` page structure;
- use the current FAR site only as evidence of brand identity, not as a layout template.

## Functional-scope guardrail

Visible concepts in the approved mockup do **not** expand the executable Interface Inventory or API contract. In particular, quote flows, payments, FAQs, public documents or customer account features shown illustratively are not new internal capabilities unless separately approved through Blueprint scope/API governance.

The authoritative internal WEB-001 destinations remain the approved executable backlog:

- start claim -> WEB-002;
- track claim -> WEB-006;
- operator login -> WEB-008.
